One image that sums up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026: a
one-page newspaper front page, "THE WORLD", with the six stories that
defined the day. The image names its day in the masthead, so a reader can
tell at a glance which day it sums up.

1. **COP31 collapses** — climate talks in Antalya end with no deal after the
   EU and allies reject a final draft that never mentioned fossil fuels.
2. **Drones hit Russian warship in Benghazi** — suspected Ukrainian sea
   drones strike the corvette Merkury at the Libyan naval base; at least
   10 people hurt.
3. **Gulf war widens** — Iran hits three tankers near Hormuz and an
   industrial site in Bahrain; Houthis claim hits on a Saudi refinery.
4. **Drone flattens flats near Kyiv** — a Russian drone destroys a
   five-storey block in Dymer, 14 missing; a missile on Kryvyi Rih kills
   two, including a seven-year-old boy.
5. **Cartel boss captured** — Mexican troops seize "El Chapo Isidro"
   (Fausto Isidro Meza Flores) in Guasave, Sinaloa, after a firefight
   that leaves 11 dead.
6. **Stocks slide into correction** — the S&P 500 falls 3.7% and the
   Nasdaq 5.3% on Friday as the AI sell-off spreads worldwide.

## How it works

`entry.png` (1024×1024, 115,430 bytes) is drawn locally and shipped
with the repository. `main.ts` (Node 22 standard library only) copies it
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. The program runs with no network and
writes byte-identical output on every run. Sources for every story are
listed in `SOURCES.md`.
